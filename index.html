Work in progress, shared for feedback. The linked pages are the real HTML, so they reflow at whatever width you open them - phone included. Screens without a link are images for now.

Click through it instead of reading it: app prototypeweb prototype The tab bar, the product tabs, the tile links and the wizard all navigate. Anything not drawn yet is marked rather than dead.

Not a frozen spec. These are proposals under active iteration: the layout and the content are the point, the exact pixels are not final, and several cross-screen moves are still open. If you are implementing one, the screen's design-brief.md and implement-spec.md are what to build from - this page is the reference render.
